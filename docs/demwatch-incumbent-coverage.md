# DemWatch cohort coverage

## Newly elected (2026 cycle)
All tracked 2026 Democrat winners / leading candidates already in DemWatch: Senate, governors, and House flips/wave set.

## Already in office (incumbents)
- **Senate:** Full sitting Democratic *party* senators (45). Independents who caucus with Democrats are not listed as party Democrats.
- **Governors:** All sitting Democratic governors in the roster (24).
- **House:** Full **voting** House Democratic Caucus from [democrats.house.gov/who-we-are/our-members](https://democrats.house.gov/who-we-are/our-members) (214 members as scraped). Cross-checked against the Clerk’s Official List of Members (119th). Non-voting delegates (DC, PR, VI, etc.) are excluded from the map roster.

## Shared UI
Official pages use the same Promises | Achievements layout with **Pursuing** and **Vote history**.

**Pursuing** prioritizes controversial stated goals in their own words when available (tweets/X, interviews) and hard bill actions — especially:
- Explicit impeachment / 25th Amendment removal calls (Axios, The Hill, NBC)
- Cosponsorship of H.Res.996 to impeach DHS Sec. Kristi Noem (Ohio Capital Journal / GovInfo)
- Abolish ICE / Medicare for All platform hard goals for named progressives
- Leadership quotes (Jeffries, Raskin, Garcia, Crockett, Schumer, etc.)

Members without an attributed hard-goal quote or impeachment cosponsorship show an empty pursuing panel rather than generic “continues serving” filler. Vote history still links official roll-call trackers.
